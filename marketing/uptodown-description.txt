SmartForm is a real-time fitness assistant for Android that uses on-device pose detection and hand tracking to coach your exercise form, count your reps, and let you control everything with gestures — no buttons, no wearables, and no video ever leaves your phone.

Now fully redesigned with a dark, athletic interface and a graph-driven workout summary.

[h3]🏋️ Smart Rep Counting & Form Coaching[/h3]
- Real-time full-body pose detection (ML Kit) with a live skeleton overlay
- Counts reps for curls, squats, and push-ups
- Form gating: sloppy or partial movements don't get counted
- Per-rep quality scoring that flags shallow reps and reps done too fast

[h3]📊 Session Summary with Graphs[/h3]
- Average-score ring and total-rep hero stats
- Score-per-rep trend chart
- Good / shallow / too-fast breakdown donut
- Depth and tempo consistency graphs
- Full rep-quality timeline

[h3]🤏 Hands-Free Gesture Controls[/h3]
[b]Pinch & hold[/b] — start or stop a session
[b]Open palm & hold[/b] — switch exercise mode
✔ Debounced with multi-frame confirmation
✔ Resistant to accidental triggers
[b]New:[/b] on-screen buttons mirror every gesture, so the app stays usable even when your hands aren't in view

[h3]✋ Real-Time Hand Tracking[/h3]
- 21-point hand landmark detection (MediaPipe)
- Smooth, stabilized overlay
- Runs alongside pose detection on a single camera stream

[h3]🎯 Personal Calibration[/h3]
- Calibrate each exercise to your own range of motion
- Saved on-device; reset to defaults anytime

[h3]🔒 100% On-Device & Private[/h3]
- All pose and hand processing runs locally
- No video is ever uploaded or stored

[h3]🆕 What's New in 1.0.1[/h3]

- Pose tracking, rep counting, and on-screen controls were unaffected
